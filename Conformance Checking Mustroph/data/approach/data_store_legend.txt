- train logistic regression model on the conformance fitness results to predict whether a suffix is risky (contain deviations) or safe.
- then thresholds for deviation labels on risk examples are determined, e.g., 
    Target calibrated deviation label threshols: {('>>', 'Assign seriousness'): np.float64(0.16),
                                                  ('Create SW anomaly', '>>'): np.float64(0.0),
                                                  ('Require upgrade', '>>'): np.float64(0.02),

                                                  ('Take in charge ticket', '>>'): np.float64(0.33),
                                                  ('Wait', '>>'): np.float64(0.33)}
